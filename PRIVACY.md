# Privacy

Haulmate processes your `Game.log` **in your browser only**.

- The log never leaves your PC. There is no server, no account, no analytics and no tracking.
- The app makes no network requests except loading its own static files.
- What is stored, and where: the folder permission, your settings, confirmed cargo and your earnings history, in your browser's storage for this site. Clearing site data removes all of it.
- Haulmate only reads `Game.log`. It never writes to the game folder.

The log contains other players' names and ids. If you share a log excerpt in an issue, anonymise it first.
